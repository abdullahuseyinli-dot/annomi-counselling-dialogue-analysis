# Claim–evidence crosswalk

This crosswalk is the reporting boundary for a manuscript derived from this repository. Rounded
values below are accompanied by links to the exact machine-readable tables. A **commit-locked**
status means only that the protocol was fixed in local Git before the stated evaluation; it is not
a claim of public preregistration. **Development-consumed** evidence had already informed later
design choices. **Post-hoc** analyses were specified after the preceding results were known.
**Quarantined** records cannot enter performance evaluation.

## Prohibited extrapolations

The evidence above does not support claims of clinical validity, therapeutic effectiveness,
patient-outcome prediction, safe or optimal action selection, population-wide annotator behaviour,
state-of-the-art performance, or confirmed generalization outside the public AnnoMI demonstrations.
No manuscript wording should turn a numerical optimum, secondary endpoint, post-hoc result, or
quarantined sample into a supported primary claim.
