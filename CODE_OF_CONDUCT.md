# Code of conduct

## Our standard

Project discussions should be respectful, specific, and evidence-led. Participants are expected to
welcome constructive disagreement, credit prior work, protect sensitive data, and avoid harassment,
discrimination, intimidation, or disclosure of another person's private information.

## Scope

This standard applies in the repository's issues, reviews, discussions, and other project spaces.
Scientific criticism is welcome when it addresses methods, evidence, or claims rather than people.

## Reporting and enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately through the repository's security-advisory channel. Maintainers
will review reports confidentially where possible and may edit or remove contributions, limit
participation, or take other proportionate action. Deliberately false or retaliatory reports are
also unacceptable.

This policy is intentionally concise. It may be replaced by a recognized community code before a
larger contributor community is invited.
